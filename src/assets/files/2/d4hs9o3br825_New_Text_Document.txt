Data Science is not built on a single skill. It brings together multiple disciplines to transform raw data into meaningful insights.

The journey typically starts with two important foundations:

📌 Statistics: Understanding data, probability, distributions, hypothesis testing, and statistical analysis.

📌 Computer Science: Developing programming skills, algorithms, data structures, and computational thinking.

When these two disciplines work together, they create the foundation for Data Science.

But becoming a Data Scientist requires much more than statistics and programming.

A successful Data Scientist also needs to understand:
🔹 Data Analysis: Explore datasets and identify meaningful patterns.
🔹 Machine Learning: Build models that learn from data and make predictions.
🔹 Data Visualization: Communicate findings through clear and effective visuals.
🔹 Domain Knowledge: Understand business problems and connect data insights to real-world decisions.
🔹 Problem-Solving: Translate complex challenges into data-driven solutions.
